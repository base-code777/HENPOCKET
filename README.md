# HENPocket


A free, open-source Android companion app for **PS3 HEN** and **webMAN MOD**. Discover your console automatically on Wi-Fi, monitor live temperatures, auto-route files to the right PS3 directories over FTP, and split large files for FAT32 USB drives—all from your phone without needing a PC.


## Features
* **Auto-Discovery:** Automatically scans your local Wi-Fi subnet to find and connect to your PS3.
* **Live Telemetry:** Real-time CPU & RSX temperatures, fan speed percentage, and free `/dev_hdd0` storage.
* **webMAN Remote:** Refresh XMB, reboot, or shut down your PS3 remotely in one tap.
* **Smart FTP File Router:** Automatically sends `.pkg`, `.rap`, ISO, and homebrew files to their proper folders on `/dev_hdd0` (e.g., `/dev_hdd0/packages/`, `/dev_hdd0/exdata/`).
* **FAT32 File Splitter:** Splits files larger than 4 GB into PS3-compatible `.66600` chunks directly on your phone.
* **Extras:** Built-in DNS helper and cleanup utilities.


## Requirements
* Android device on the same local Wi-Fi network as your PS3
* PS3 running **HEN** (or CFW) with **webMAN MOD** installed and active


## Installation
1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com)** page and download **`HENPocket.apk`**.
2. Open `HENPocket.apk` on your Android phone and allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.
3. Enable HEN on your PS3, open **HENPocket**, and connect.
